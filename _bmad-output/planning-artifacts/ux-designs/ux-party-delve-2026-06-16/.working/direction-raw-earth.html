Direction 4 of 4 — Raw Earth

This direction strips sentiment away entirely: terracotta on brown-earth, monospace type, and a roster grid that reads like field orders. The corruption in this world isn't eldritch — it's hostile terrain. Every element earns its pixel; the UI looks like it was built by someone who needed it to work, not to impress. The hub feels like a military field camp, not a fantasy inn.
